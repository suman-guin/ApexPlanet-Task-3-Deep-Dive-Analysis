Key Business Insights

  • High Value customers contribute 55.27% of total sales while representing approximately one quarter of the customer base.
  • Electronics is the leading category with 36.43% of total sales.
  • Patna leads total city sales (19,285,966.89).
  • Bengaluru has the highest sales per customer (156,446.45).
  • March 2025 is the strongest full month in the analysis period (13,059,899.94).
  • Low Value customers represent approximately one quarter of customers but contribute only around 4% of sales (4.04%).

Insights are stated for the full dataset and validated against the Task 3 analysis files.

Business Recommendations

  • Protect and retain High Value customers.
  • Develop suitable Low Value customers through targeted offers, cross-selling and upselling.
  • Monitor Electronics performance closely.
  • Compare city performance using both total sales and sales per customer.
  • Monitor monthly sales fluctuations.
  • Investigate repeated Order IDs before operational order-level reporting.
